Ten generations · 1700 – 2026

The Lengyel Line

Czech Protestants who left Bohemia when their faith was outlawed, founded a town in Poland, farmed the Volhynian steppe for eighty years, sent a branch to the Canadian prairies — and came home to a borderland emptied by war.
